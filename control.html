The drive starts when the participant presses the accelerator. The street starts empty so they can get used to the wheel; add road users below when they're ready. Pause and Restart work as usual.

Navigation · next turn

A 7 × 7 city grid of two-way streets (parking · bike lane · oncoming lane | your lane · bike lane · parking), a junction every 110 m, closed by a wall of buildings so every street ends in a dead end. Left and right turns work at every junction; the cluster arrow shows the turn you pick here. The car keeps its lane; turning the wheel in the last ~20 m before a junction makes the turn. At a dead end the car stops: press Restart. Indicators: wheel buttons, or Q / E on the windscreen keyboard.
